What are Cake Meetings?

Cake Meetings are our monthly WPE seminar and community gatherings bringing together academic staff, postdoctoral researchers, and PhD students from across all four core research themes (Indoor Air Quality & Airborne Infection, Water Supply & Treatment, Sanitation, Wastewater & Faecal Sludge Management, and BioResources & the Circular Economy) and our cross-cutting Data-Driven & Computational Methods theme for knowledge sharing, interdisciplinary discussion, and community building, always with cake in the room.

Cake Meetings are run jointly by academic and Early Career Researcher (ECR) staff. Each session pairs an academic host with an ECR or doctoral researcher co-host, overseen by WPE Director Dr Marco-Felipe King and Deputy Director Dr Amirul Khan.

Session Formats

Interdisciplinary Sandpits

Cross-theme discussions where researchers from Indoor Air Quality, Water Supply & Treatment, Sanitation, and BioResources tackle shared methodological or global challenges.

Grants, Fellowships & Careers

Practical sessions on fellowship applications, grant writing, peer review, and career pathways across academia, policy, utilities, and industry.

Fieldwork, Safety & Ethics

Sharing best practice from international fieldwork, Containment Level 2 (CL2) laboratory biosafety, equitable partnerships, and community engagement.

Method & Theme Deep-Dives

Technical sessions showcasing emerging methods, including Digital Twins, physics-informed neural operators, CFD surrogates, microfluidics, and circular bioresources.

Flagship Event: WPE All-Hands in Leeds Week (10–14 May 2027)

From Monday 10 May to Friday 14 May 2027, WPE will host our flagship All-Hands in Leeds Week at the School of Civil Engineering, scaling the cohort model established by the EPSRC WaterWISER CDT across our entire group of academic staff, postdoctoral fellows, technical staff, and doctoral researchers. Built around a daily 11:00 AM coffee anchor and one flagship session per day alongside unstructured time for supervision and collaboration, the week includes:

Mon 10 & Tue 11 May: Welcome, Lab Tours & School PGR Conference

Kick-off 11:00 AM All-Hands Coffee and rotating "Behind the Labs & Models" tours on Monday, followed on Tuesday by the all-day School of Civil Engineering PGR Conference showcasing WPE doctoral talks and posters.

Wed 12 May: CDT Training Grants Panel & Invited Keynote

Morning panel on winning and delivering large doctoral training grants (drawing on lessons from the WaterWISER and Future Fluid Dynamics CDTs), followed by an afternoon flagship invited narrative keynote seminar.

Thu 13 May: WPE Annual General Meeting (AGM) & Social

Afternoon WPE AGM covering research culture, theme and centre spotlights, facilities updates, and ECR/PGR voice, followed from 17:00 by the WPE Summer Social.

Fri 14 May: Wrap-Up Coffee & Unstructured Collaboration

Morning 11:00 AM wrap-up coffee followed by protected unstructured time for co-supervisor catch-ups, ad-hoc grant and paper writing groups, and laboratory or code troubleshooting.
