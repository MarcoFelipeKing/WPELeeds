What are Cake Meetings?

Cake Meetings are our regular WPE seminar and community gatherings bringing together academic staff, postdoctoral researchers, and PhD students from across all four research themes for knowledge sharing, interdisciplinary discussion, and community building — always with cake in the room.

For the 2026–2027 academic year, every session pairs a named academic host with a named postgraduate or postdoctoral co-host, coordinated by our PGR Cake Meeting Leads Bushra Hasan (cnbh@leeds.ac.uk) and Anushi Khandare alongside WPE Director Associate Professor Marco-Felipe King.

Session Formats

Interdisciplinary Sandpits

Cross-theme discussions where researchers from Indoor Air, Water, Sanitation, and BioResources tackle shared methodological or global challenges.

Grants, Fellowships & Careers

Practical sessions on fellowship applications, grant writing, peer review, and career pathways across academia, policy, utilities, and industry.

Fieldwork, Safety & Ethics

Sharing best practice from international fieldwork, laboratory biosafety (CL2), equitable partnerships, and community engagement.

Method & Theme Deep-Dives

Technical sessions showcasing emerging methods — from Digital Twins, PINNs, and CFD surrogates to microfluidics and circular bioresources.

Recent Session Highlight: 3 November 2026

COMPLETED • 3 NOVEMBER 2026

Digital Twins & AI in Water, Public Health and Environmental Engineering

Academic Chair: Associate Professor Amirul Khan (Deputy Director of WPE; Director, Centre for Computational Engineering)
Panel Contributors: Dr Maryam Asachi & Dr Xiaohui Chen

Our opening session of the 2026–2027 series examined how physics-informed machine learning (PINNs), reduced-order CFD surrogates, chemometric data fusion, and Bayesian sensor assimilation are enabling operational digital twins across hospital wards, wastewater treatment plants, and subsurface groundwater systems.

Flagship Event: WPE All-Hands in Leeds Week (10–14 May 2027)

From Monday 10 May to Friday 14 May 2027, WPE will host our annual All-Hands in Leeds Week at the School of Civil Engineering. Designed to bring together our Leeds-based staff and doctoral researchers with our international collaborators, visiting professors, alumni, and industry/utility partners, the week anchors our group's strategic planning and external engagement.

Day 1–2: Theme Strategy & Sandpits

Research theme reviews across Indoor Air, Water, Sanitation, and BioResources, alongside cross-cutting proposal sandpits for UKRI, Horizon Europe, FCDO, and Wellcome calls.

Day 3: Partner Showcase & Lab Tours

Guided demonstrations of the walk-in Aerobiology Chamber, the HACH AP3900 analytical suite, and our bioprocess and hydraulic laboratories for industry, NHS, and utility guests.

Day 4: PGR & PDRA Conference

Doctoral and postdoctoral lightning talks, poster competition, and career panels with WPE alumni working in global health, water utilities, consultancy, and academia.

Day 5: Plenary Cake Meeting & Social

Keynote lecture from our visiting international fellows followed by the annual WPE Plenary Cake Meeting and group celebration.

External partners and alumni wishing to attend or contribute to the May 2027 All-Hands Week are warmly invited to contact Associate Professor Marco-Felipe King.
