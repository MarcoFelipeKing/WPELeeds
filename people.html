How our themes collaborate

Who actually writes with whom. Each line is co-authorship between two of our four research themes, counted from our research outputs for 2018 to 2025 and including our doctoral researchers, who are placed with their supervisor. The thin connections matter as much as the thick ones: they are where a cross-theme collaboration is only just beginning.

Dr Marco-Felipe King

Director of WPE; Lecturer in Building Engineering and Infection Transmission; Co-Director, EPSRC CDT in Future Fluid Dynamics
Indoor Air Quality & Airborne Infection

Combines quantitative microbial risk assessment (QMRA) with computational fluid dynamics and experiments in the UK's largest controlled aerobiology chamber to model pathogen transmission across air, hands, and surfaces.

Indoor Air Theme

Dr Amirul Khan

Deputy Director of WPE; Associate Professor in Environmental Fluid Mechanics; Co-Lead, LIFD Computational Group
Indoor Air Quality & Airborne Infection

Leads computational fluid dynamics research across building ventilation, urban air quality, and multiphase environmental flows, developing reduced-order models and physics-informed machine learning surrogates for real-time flow prediction.
