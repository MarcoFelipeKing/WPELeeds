WPE Member Survey Results

Insights and analysis from our 2025 research community survey

Thank you to everyone who participated in our recent survey. Your feedback has been invaluable in understanding how we can better serve our research community. This page summarizes the key findings and our planned actions moving forward.

Survey Response Profile

Respondent Demographics

Key Engagement Metrics

Name Representation
"Too big to provide community, too small for external profile."

This insight from one respondent highlights a key structural challenge we need to address in our strategic planning.

Most Desired Activities

What Members Seek from WPE

Four primary themes emerged regarding what members hope to gain:

  • Community Building - "A sense of community" and "Re-establishing network of WPE staff and students"
  • Collaboration Opportunities - "Grow opportunities for collaborative research proposals and papers"
  • Skills Development - Research skills and professional development
  • Identity and Recognition - "A research centre that gives an identity for international exposure"

Preferred Meeting Types

Research Keywords

The word frequency analysis revealed our group's diverse research interests:

Water treatment Public health Sanitation Environmental engineering Climate change Air quality Microbiology Governance Hydraulics Gender Modelling Emissions Contamination

This diverse set of research interests provides numerous opportunities for interdisciplinary collaboration within WPE.

Implementation Plan

Based on your feedback, we are developing a three-phase implementation plan:

Phase 1
May-July 2025
Horizons Scanning & Grant-writing Workshops
Phase 2
Aug-Oct 2025
Themed Networking & Skill-building Sessions
Phase 3
Nov-Dec 2025
WPE Annual Awards & Identity Building

Immediate Next Steps

  • May 15th: First Grant-writing Workshop focusing on UKRI opportunities
  • May 30th: Themed Networking event on "Water Treatment Innovations"
  • June: Launch of working groups around core research themes
