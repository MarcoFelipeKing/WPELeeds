Upcoming WPE Events

Cake Meeting: Building Your Academic Profile

April 25th, 2025 11:30 AM - 12:30 PM Hybrid (Teams and 4.18 Civil Engineering)

Whether you're just starting out or looking to strengthen your academic presence, this session is packed with actionable insights to help you elevate your profile.

This interactive session will be led by Prof Cath Noakes, Pro-Dean for Research and Innovation in the Faculty, and will cover:

  • Motivations
  • University expectations
  • Symplectic and University web profiles
  • Open Access
  • ORCID, Google scholar, Scopus
  • Promoting work, including the press office and on social media
And yes, there will be cake! 🍰 So join us in person if you can.
