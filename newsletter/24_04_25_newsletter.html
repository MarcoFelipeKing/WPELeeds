Actions Moving Forward

Research Clusters

Creating smaller thematic groups within WPE to facilitate deeper connections among researchers with aligned interests.

Skills Development Series

Quarterly workshops focused on grant writing, academic profile building, and other professional skills identified in the survey.

Enhanced Communication

Launching a monthly newsletter and improving our website to better showcase member research and achievements.

Community Building

Implementing more regular social gatherings and networking opportunities, both formal and informal.

External Profile Building

Developing an annual report highlighting WPE research impact and exploring opportunities for joint publications.
